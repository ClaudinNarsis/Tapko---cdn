FAQ

Common questions

Can't find what you need? Email us at support@acme.dev.

Can I switch plans mid-cycle?
Yes. You can upgrade instantly and we'll prorate the difference. Downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period.
Is there a free trial for Pro?
Yes — all Pro features are available free for 14 days. No credit card required to start.
How does per-seat pricing work?
You're billed for the number of active users in your workspace. Guests and viewers are never counted.
What payment methods do you accept?
Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and ACH bank transfer. Enterprise customers can also pay by invoice.
Can I export my data if I cancel?
Absolutely. You can export all tasks, comments, attachments, and history at any time in JSON or CSV format.
