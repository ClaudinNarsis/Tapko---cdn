Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate any charges.

What happens if I exceed my monthly limit?

We'll send you a notification when you reach 80% of your limit. You can upgrade your plan or we'll automatically archive older feedback to keep you within limits.

Is there a long-term contract?

No long-term contracts required! All plans are month-to-month. We also offer annual billing with a 20% discount.

Do you offer non-profit discounts?

Yes! We offer 50% off Professional plans for eligible non-profit organizations. Contact us for details.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers for Enterprise customers.
