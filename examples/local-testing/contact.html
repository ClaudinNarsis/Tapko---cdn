Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I get a response?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent technical issues, email support@tapko.io directly.

Do you offer phone support?

Phone support is available for Enterprise customers. Professional plan customers can schedule calls with our support team on a case-by-case basis.

Can I schedule a demo?

Absolutely! Select "Sales Inquiry" in the form above and mention you'd like a demo. We'll get back to you within one business day to schedule a time.

Where are you located?

We're a distributed team with headquarters in San Francisco, CA. Our team members work from around the world to provide global support.
