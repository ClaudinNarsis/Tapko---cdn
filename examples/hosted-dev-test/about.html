About Tapko

Building the future of user feedback, one interaction at a time

Our Mission

We believe that every user's voice matters. Our mission is to make feedback collection effortless, insightful, and actionable for businesses of all sizes. By removing barriers between users and product teams, we help companies build products people truly love.

Our Story

Tapko was founded in 2023 by a team of product managers and engineers who were frustrated with traditional feedback tools. They were clunky, expensive, and often required users to navigate away from the product they were using.

We set out to build something different - a feedback tool that feels native to your application, requires minimal setup, and provides actionable insights from day one.

Today, thousands of companies trust Tapko to collect and analyze user feedback, helping them make better product decisions faster.
